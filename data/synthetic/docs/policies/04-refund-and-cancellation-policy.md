# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Monthly subscriptions may be refunded within the first 14 days of service. Annual subscriptions
are eligible for a prorated refund minus any usage for the months already consumed.

## Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days after approval. The refund will be issued
to the original payment method used for the purchase.

## 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

CloudDesk stands behind its platform with a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you are not
satisfied with CloudDesk for any reason within the first 30 days of your initial subscription,
contact our billing team at billing@clouddesk.com to request a full refund. No questions asked.

## Tier-Based Refund Schedule

Refund eligibility depends on your plan tier and billing cycle:

- **Monthly subscriptions**: Full refund available within the first 14 days of each billing
  cycle. After 14 days, refunds are prorated based on the remaining days in the billing period.
- **Annual subscriptions**: Eligible for a prorated refund for unused months, minus a 10%
  administrative fee. No refund is issued after 6 months of an annual term have elapsed.
- **Enterprise contracts**: Refund terms are defined in your signed enterprise agreement.
  Contact your account executive for details specific to your contract.

## How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your CloudDesk account at app.clouddesk.com.
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Payment History**.
3. Locate the charge you wish to dispute and click **Request Refund**.
4. Select the reason for your refund request from the dropdown menu.
5. Submit the request. Our billing team will review it within 2 business days.

Alternatively, email billing@clouddesk.com with your account email, the invoice number,
and a brief explanation of your refund request. Premium and Enterprise customers may also
contact their dedicated account manager for expedited processing.

## What Happens After Approval

Once your refund is approved:

- The funds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
- Your subscription is cancelled effective immediately unless you specify otherwise.
- Access to CloudDesk features continues until the end of the current billing period.
- A confirmation email with the refund receipt is sent to your registered email address.

## Exceptions and Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

- Add-on purchases and third-party integration fees.
- Setup, onboarding, or professional services fees already delivered.
- Charges older than 120 days from the request date.
- Partial-month usage after cancellation (billed at the daily prorated rate).
