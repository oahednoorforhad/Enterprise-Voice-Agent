# Getting Started with CloudDesk

## Introduction

Welcome to CloudDesk CRM. This guide walks you through the initial setup process so you can start
managing your customer relationships effectively. CloudDesk provides a unified platform for tracking
leads, managing deals, and delivering support across multiple channels.

## System Requirements

Before installing CloudDesk, ensure your environment meets the following minimum requirements.
The platform supports all modern web browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
For the mobile app, devices must run iOS 14 or Android 11 and above.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Visit app.clouddesk.com and click **Start Free Trial**. Enter your work email address,
choose a strong password (minimum 12 characters with at least one uppercase letter and one
number), and select your company name and industry. A verification email will be sent to
your inbox — click the link to activate your account.

Once verified, you will be prompted to complete your profile: full name, job title, and
phone number. We recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) from the Security
settings page before proceeding.

## Step 2: Invite Your Team

After setting up your profile, invite team members to join your CloudDesk organization:

1. Navigate to **Settings** → **Team Management** → **Invite Members**.
2. Enter the email addresses of your colleagues, one per line or comma-separated.
3. Assign each invitee a role: **Administrator**, **Manager**, **Agent**, or **Viewer**.
   You can customise role permissions later from the Roles & Permissions page.
4. Click **Send Invitations**. Each recipient receives a welcome email with a unique
   sign-up link. Invitations expire after 7 days.

We recommend adding at least one backup administrator so account access is never lost.

## Step 3: Configure Your Workspace

CloudDesk adapts to your sales process. Spend a few minutes on initial configuration:

- **Sales Pipelines**: Go to **Pipeline Settings** and create pipelines that mirror your
  sales stages — for example, Lead → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won.
  Each stage can be assigned a probability percentage for revenue forecasting.
- **Custom Fields**: Add fields specific to your business, such as industry vertical,
  lead source, or product interest. Custom fields are available on contacts, deals,
  and tickets.
- **Email Integration**: Connect your corporate email (Gmail, Outlook, or any IMAP server)
  so all customer conversations are captured in CloudDesk automatically. Go to
  **Settings** → **Email Configuration** and follow the OAuth setup wizard.
- **Notification Rules**: Define who receives alerts for new tickets, deal stage changes,
  and invoice events. Configure quiet hours to avoid after-hours notifications.

## Step 4: Import Existing Data

If you are migrating from another CRM or spreadsheet, CloudDesk provides several
import options:

- **CSV Import**: Go to **Data Management** → **Import** and upload your contacts, deals,
  or tickets as CSV files. Use the column mapping tool to match your fields to CloudDesk
  data fields. A preview is shown before the import is finalised.
- **Direct Migration**: For migrations from Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho, use our
  one-click migration wizard under **Settings** → **Migrations**. Your data — including
  historical notes and attachments — is transferred with full fidelity.
- **API Import**: Developers can use the CloudDesk REST API to programmatically insert
  or update records. See the API Reference Guide for endpoint documentation.

All imports are processed in the background. You will receive an email notification when
the import is complete.

## Step 5: Create Your First Project

Organise your work into projects. Each project contains its own deals, contacts, tickets,
and dashboard:

1. Click **+ New Project** from the top navigation bar.
2. Enter a project name and description (e.g., "Q3 Product Launch").
3. Select the pipeline stages that apply to this project.
4. Assign team members and set project visibility (private or organisation-wide).

Projects keep your team focused and make it easy to track progress toward specific
business objectives.

## Step 6: Connect Integrations

CloudDesk connects with hundreds of third-party tools through its integration marketplace.
Popular integrations include:

- **Slack**: Receive deal updates, ticket assignments, and report alerts directly in your
  Slack channels.
- **Salesforce**: Sync contacts, accounts, and opportunities bidirectionally.
- **QuickBooks / Xero**: Automatically sync invoices and payment records.
- **Mailchimp**: Sync contact lists and campaign performance data.
- **Zapier**: Connect CloudDesk to over 2000 other apps with no-code automation.

Browse the full catalog from **Settings** → **Integrations** and click **Connect** on any
integration to begin the OAuth setup flow.

  your dashboard.

- **Live Chat**: Available from the bottom-right corner of the CloudDesk web app.
- **Phone Support**: +1 (800) 555-CLOUD — available for Premium and Enterprise plans.
- **Developer Docs**: Complete API reference and SDK documentation at
  developers.clouddesk.com.

New customers are also eligible for a complimentary onboarding session with a CloudDesk
